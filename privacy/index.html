Privacy Policy

Cleo Workspace Bridge · Last updated: September 11, 2026

Cleo Workspace Bridge (the “App”) is a private, single-user command-line tool used by its owner to read and manage his own Google Workspace data. It is not offered as a service to the public and has no users other than its owner.

What the App accesses

With the account owner’s explicit permission, the App uses Google APIs to access the following data belonging to the owner’s own Google Account(s):

  • Gmail — read, search, and send messages
  • Google Drive — read and manage files
  • Google Calendar — read and manage events
  • Google Docs and Sheets — read, export, and create documents and spreadsheets
  • Google Contacts — read contacts

How the data is used

Accessed data is used solely to carry out tasks the owner requests (for example, searching email, summarizing documents, or scheduling events). The App does not use this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the owner’s requests.

How the data is stored

OAuth credentials (tokens) are stored locally on the owner’s own devices and are not transmitted to, or stored on, any third-party server operated by the App. Google user data is processed on the owner’s devices and is not retained beyond what is necessary to complete a requested task.

Sharing

The App does not share, sell, or transfer Google user data to any third party. No person other than the owner reads the data.

Limited Use disclosure

The App’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the App’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored credentials can be deleted at any time, and any data cached by the App is deleted once the task is complete.
